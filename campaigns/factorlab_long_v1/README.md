# Long-horizon Neural FactorLab v1

This is the active research benchmark. Its anchor has 5,000 decisions, a
10^12 factored joint-action space, terminal-only two-component reward,
continuous procedural observations, a suite-shared hidden nonlinear kernel,
and compact neural learners. A 20,000-step contrast is mandatory evidence for
scaling and causal-span claims.

Only tiers listed in `definition.json` under `admitted_tiers` may be used by
the automated research controller. The retired 64-step suite is smoke-only.
